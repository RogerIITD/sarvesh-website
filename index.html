So, who's this guy?

I've never really been a one lane kind of person. Right now I'm spending most of my time with AI, playing around with tools like Claude Code and Codex, trying to turn random ideas into actual features and products.

I recently interviewed for a GTM AI builder role at a major FinTech unicorn and it made one thing clear to me: I want to work where technology meets go-to-market strategy.

I'm also not just a tech person. I spent time preparing for UPSC and that shaped how I think. Multidisciplinary, curious, always connecting dots across history, economics, and culture. I love finding patterns in unexpected places.

I'm looking for the right opportunity and the right people to work with. If you're someone who thinks about this stuff too, let's talk.
